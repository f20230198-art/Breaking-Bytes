# Style tests: hero scene

Paste each prompt into ChatGPT as a **new chat**, landscape 1536x1024. Same scene every time, so only the style changes.
Pick the one you like, then we generate the hero pieces separately in that style.

Scene used by all tests (don't edit):
> A wide desert valley at dusk. On the LEFT third: a battered 1980s white RV with a tan stripe, door ajar with cold blue light inside, two folding camp chairs and a gas mask on the ground beside it. Far behind: flat-topped mesas and a low setting sun. A tumbleweed mid-roll, a few small black birds in the sky. The RIGHT half of the image is open sky and empty desert (space for a title). No text, no people, no logos.

---

## A. Dark screen-print poster (recommended)
```
[scene]. Style: limited-edition screen-printed movie poster, 5 flat ink colors only: near-black, deep indigo night sky, burnt orange, dusty cream, and one glowing cyan for the RV doorway. Hard-edged flat shapes, no gradients, no soft shading, crisp clean silhouettes, subtle ink grain. Dark, moody, cinematic, high contrast. Inspired by Mondo-style alternative posters.
```

## B. Dark linocut (current style, at night)
```
[scene]. Style: hand-carved linocut block print, thick black carved lines and gouge marks, printed on cream paper, only 3 inks: black, deep burnt orange, and one cyan accent for the RV doorway. Black dominates the image (70%), dusk is almost night. Bold, crisp, high contrast, visible carving texture.
```

## C. Scratchboard / engraving
```
[scene]. Style: scratchboard engraving, white fine-line hatching scratched out of solid black, like an old banknote or woodcut engraving. Monochrome black and bone-white, with ONLY the RV doorway glow in cyan and the sun in dull orange. Extremely crisp lines, dramatic, dark.
```

## D. Risograph duotone
```
[scene]. Style: risograph print, two inks only (deep teal-black and fluorescent orange) on black paper, with a cyan spot color for the RV doorway. Flat shapes, slight misregistration, fine riso grain, crisp edges, moody dusk.
```

## E. Neo-western graphic novel
```
[scene]. Style: dark graphic-novel art in the style of a modern western, heavy black spot shadows, flat limited colors (dusk purple, rust orange, sand), sharp clean ink edges, no gradients, no realism. Brooding, cinematic.
```

---

After you pick: the next prompts will generate each piece **alone on a flat #FF00FF magenta background**
(RV, RV-interior, chairs + gas mask, tumbleweed, birds, mesas, sun, sky, ground). Those get converted to SVG,
so they stay sharp at any size.
