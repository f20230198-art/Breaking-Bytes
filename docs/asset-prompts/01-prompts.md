# Per-asset prompts (prepend the style bible from 00-style-bible.md)

## hero-rv ← GENERATE THIS FIRST (style test)
A battered 1980s RV camper parked alone in the open New Mexico desert at
golden hour, 3/4 side view, the door ajar with a faint crystal-blue glow
spilling out, a thin wisp of smoke from the roof vent, two folding chairs and
a discarded gas mask in the dirt, distant mesas sketched lightly in pencil.
Vignette fading into cream paper at the edges. Landscape 3:2.

## hero-mesas / hero-foreground (parallax layers, after the style is locked)
Same scene split into layers on plain cream paper with empty margins so they
can be cut out: (a) distant mesas strip, 3:1; (b) foreground cracked earth,
sagebrush, chair with a draped yellow hazmat suit, 3:1.

## about-chalkboard
A high-school chemistry classroom drawn in charcoal: a big dusty chalkboard
covered in formulas and molecule sketches, a teacher in a short-sleeve shirt
seen from behind holding chalk, a periodic table poster on the wall. Leave the
centre of the chalkboard mostly clear (text goes there in code). 2:1.

## prop-handshake
Interior of the cramped RV lab, low angle: two figures in respirators and
hazmat suits shaking hands over a folding table with a laptop showing a
glowing blue screen, lab glassware around. 4:3.

## prize-barrel (×3) + prize-barrel-student (×2)

banded cash stacks inside, a paper luggage tag tied to it (blank). Drawn on
plain cream paper with empty margins for cutout. 4:5.

## timeline-01 … 05 (sketchbook panels, 9:7 each)
1. A flashy lawyer's strip-mall office: desk covered in flip phones, a hand fanning them out toward the viewer (no face visible).
2. Gloved hands opening a manila folder of chemical diagrams under a desk lamp.
3. Night inside the lab: two figures in respirators at laptops glowing blue, beakers, a wall clock.
4. Dawn in the desert: a gloved hand hiding a USB drive in a duffel bag under a flat rock.
5. Three people in lab coats and goggles, faces shadowed, examining one glowing blue crystal under a lamp.

rivets, cross-hatched shading. Cream paper margins. 1:1. Then the spoked hand
wheel alone, front view, 1:1 (it gets rotated in code).
