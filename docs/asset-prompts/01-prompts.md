# Per-asset prompts (prepend the style bible from 00-style-bible.md)

## hero-rv ← GENERATE THIS FIRST (style test)
A battered 1980s RV camper parked alone in the open New Mexico desert at
golden hour, 3/4 side view, the door ajar with a faint crystal-blue glow
spilling out, a thin wisp of smoke from the roof vent, two folding chairs and
a discarded gas mask in the dirt, distant mesas sketched lightly in pencil.
Vignette fading into cream paper at the edges. Landscape 3:2.

## hero-mesas / hero-foreground (parallax layers, after the style is locked)
Same scene split into layers on plain cream paper with empty margins so they
can be cut out: (a) distant mesas strip, 3:1; (b) foreground cracked earth,
sagebrush, chair with a draped yellow hazmat suit, 3:1.

## about-chalkboard

covered in formulas and molecule sketches, a teacher in a short-sleeve shirt
seen from behind holding chalk, a periodic table poster on the wall. Leave the
centre of the chalkboard mostly clear (text goes there in code). 2:1.

glowing blue screen, lab glassware around. 4:3.

## fuse-desert-tile (timeline background, behind the fuse)
In the spirit of Breaking Bad: the New Mexico desert outside Albuquerque
where the cooks hide out. Top-down aerial view looking straight down at
the desert floor at night. Cracked dry earth, soft sand ripples, a few
small rocks, two or three small cacti and scrub bushes seen from above, a
pair of RV tyre tracks curving gently through, a dropped gas-mask
respirator half in the sand. Sparse, calm, lots of open ground; nothing
large or busy. Mostly dark: black ink with dusty orange sand texture
carved through it. A few tiny crystal-blue shards glinting in the sand.
The image must TILE SEAMLESSLY top-to-bottom: the bottom edge continues
into the top edge. Keep the centre column calmer than the sides (a fuse
and cards go there). No characters, no logos, no text. Portrait 2:3.

## prize-dig (the payout, where the fuse ends)
In the spirit of Breaking Bad's buried desert money barrels. Top-down view
straight down into a freshly dug hole in desert sand at night: five
chemical drums buried upright side by side, lids removed, each packed with
banded stacks of cash seen from above. Three blue drums (slightly larger,
centre) and two dusty olive-green drums (smaller, at the ends). A shovel
lying on the sand at the edge, a pork-pie hat dropped beside it, scattered
loose bills, dug sand piled around the rim. Blank paper luggage tags tied
to each drum (left empty; amounts are added in code). Bold, simple shapes;
the drums and cash read clearly at a glance. No characters, no logos, no
text. Landscape 16:9, generous dark sand margin all round (it gets
cropped).

## prize-room-big3 + prize-room-students (replaces prize-dig)
Two scenes in the same room. Attach the character reference sheet (flat
faceless Breaking Bad figures) to every prompt that contains a figure. No
barrels: just cash. Blank luggage tags only; amounts are added in code.

### Master 1: prize-office-big3 (v2: Saul's office)
In the spirit of Breaking Bad: a sleazy strip-mall lawyer's office at night,
redrawn as linocut. Straight-on, slightly wide view of the room. Wallpaper
printed with old parchment constitution text, a big wooden desk centre-back
with a tall leather executive chair, an American-style flag on a stand, a
cork board covered in pinned papers and red string on the right wall, a
long dark leather sofa on the left. Warm desk-lamp light, dark corners.
Figures match the outfits of the attached reference, FACELESS (plain
skin-tone head shapes, no eyes, nose or mouth), redrawn as linocut:
- CENTRE: the bald chemist (glasses, goatee, navy jacket, khakis) sitting
  back in the big leather chair behind the desk like he owns the place,
  hands resting on the armrests.
- LEFT: the calm businessman sitting upright on the leather sofa, legs
  crossed. Brown skin, short tightly cropped dark hair greying at the
  sides, thin rimless glasses, clean-shaven, grey blazer over a pale
  yellow shirt and tie. Very still and composed.
- RIGHT: the lawyer standing beside the cork board, one hand pointing at
  it, loud patterned tie, slicked-back brown hair, dark suit.
In front of each figure on the floor, one closed-ish suitcase lying flat
with the lid slightly open and a few banded cash bundles spilling out:
centre suitcase biggest, left medium, right smallest. No tags, no labels.
Keep all figures and suitcases inside the middle ~70% width x ~75% height;
fill the border with more wall and floor (it gets cropped).
No logos, no text, no readable writing, no real faces. Landscape 16:9.
