# Style Bible: "Heisenberg's Notebook" (paste at the top of EVERY image prompt)

```
STYLE: Hand-drawn realist illustration, like a film storyboard artist's
sketchbook or a forensic courtroom sketch. Graphite pencil and charcoal
drawing with loose black ink linework, cross-hatching for shadows, visible
construction lines and smudges, rough unfinished edges that fade into the
paper. Muted watercolour / marker washes applied loosely, not filling the
lines perfectly. Realistic proportions and lighting. Gritty, cinematic,
dry New Mexico atmosphere, like the TV drama Breaking Bad but hand-drawn.
NOT cartoon, NOT comic book, NOT anime, NOT 3D, NOT photorealistic, no halftone dots.
PAPER: off-white cream sketchbook paper (#EFE8D6) with subtle grain.
PALETTE: mostly desaturated: charcoal black, sepia, dusty ochre desert
(#C99A4E), faded olive / toxic green (#2E6B3F). The ONLY saturated colour is
a vivid crystal blue (#13C8E8), used only on crystals, glowing screens or
chemical liquid, so it pops against the muted drawing.
RULES: no text or lettering, no logos, no real actors or celebrity
likenesses. People are seen from behind, in silhouette, faces in shadow, or
wearing gas masks / respirators. No drugs shown; "crystals" are blue gems.
```

Append per-asset: subject, composition, background ("on plain cream paper, generous empty margins" for cut-outs; "full-bleed vignette fading to paper" for scenes), aspect ratio.

**Test this first:** generate `hero-rv` only. If it looks right, lock the style and attach that image as a reference to every later prompt.
